This Privacy Policy explains how the "Calculator" mobile application for Android (the "App"), published by DaroDev ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. The App is a scientific calculator. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. We do not operate our own servers that collect or store your personal data.

The App stores your calculation history and your angle-mode setting (degrees or radians) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete the history at any time using the "Clear" button in the History screen, or by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

This information is used to serve ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse, and, where you have given consent, to personalize ads. We do not receive your personal data from AdMob and we do not sell your personal data.
