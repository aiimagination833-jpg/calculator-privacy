This Privacy Policy explains how the "Calculator" mobile application for Android (the "App"), published by DaroDev ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. The App is a scientific calculator. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

We do not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The App does not require an account, does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, contacts, photos or location, and does not connect to the internet. We do not operate servers that receive data from the App.

The App stores your calculation history and your angle-mode setting (degrees or radians) locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete the history at any time using the "Clear" button in the History screen, or by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App.

The App does not contain advertising and does not include third-party analytics or tracking tools. No information about you is shared with third parties.

Because the App stores its data only on your device, you control how long it is kept. We rely on the security features of your device and operating system to protect that data.
